About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, in the Interactions Lab with Dr. Fateme Rajabiyazdi, working on AI and human-centered data visualization for health.

As an undergraduate I worked in the DANSA Lab with Dr. Reda Alhajj and Dr. Jon George Rokne. I studied when and why language models hallucinate, where small changes in how a question is phrased can move error rates by an order of magnitude. I also built interpretable models for early Alzheimer's detection from MRI, aiming for explanations a clinician could actually act on, and a CNN method for dating lunar surfaces from images. What carries across is making a system's behavior legible to the people who have to rely on it.

When I'm not doing research, I write about technology and my experience in research on Substack, make art, curate photography, write poems, and build tools and projects that come out of my research.
